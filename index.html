Reedr: FB2 Reader

Picks the thread of the chapter back up for you.

Reading on iPhone and iPad. Offline.

Put a book down for a week. Pick it up mid-chapter.

You know the feeling: you were four pages into a chapter, something interrupted, and now three names have turned up and you are not sure which of them stole the ledger.

Reedr keeps a short account of what has happened since the current chapter began, up to exactly where you are. It sits on one line at the top of the page. Tap it and you get the whole recap, along with every character who has appeared in it and what they were doing.

It is written while you read, never in front of you: the line appears when it is ready and nothing waits for it.

Features

📖

So Far in This Chapter

A recap from the chapter’s first line to your place in it, with the cast.

🧠

Written on Your Device

Apple Intelligence writes it locally. Your book stays with you.

📚

Real FB2 Files

Plain .fb2 and zipped books, including the windows-1251 ones.

🔖

Chapters and Parts

The table of contents exactly as the book nests it, and your place kept.

🔤

Set the Page

Serif or sans, your size, your line spacing, light or dark.

✈️

No Account, No Network

Nothing to sign in to. Works with the radio off.

Books, your place in them and every recap stay on your device.
