Common questions

How do I add a book?

Tap + in the library and pick an .fb2 file, or open one from Files, Mail or Safari and choose Reedr from the share sheet. Reedr keeps its own copy, so the book stays readable even if you move or delete the original.

My book is a .zip — will it open?

Yes. Zipped FB2 books open directly; Reedr finds the book inside the archive.

The text came out as gibberish

That would be a bug, not your file. FB2 books are often written in windows-1251 rather than Unicode and Reedr handles that, but the format is used loosely in the wild. Email a description of what you saw and which book it was, and it will be looked at.

Where is the recap?

Two reasons it may not be there yet:

  • You are near the start of the chapter. There is nothing to remind you of that is not still on the screen, so Reedr stays quiet.
  • It is still being written. The recap is made while you read, on the device, and on a long chapter the first one takes a little time. The line appears by itself when it is ready — reading is never held up for it.

Why is my recap shorter than the screenshots?

Written recaps use Apple Intelligence, which needs a recent device and iOS 26. Without it, Reedr still builds a recap — it picks out the chapter’s own sentences and the names in them. It reads differently, and it works on every device Reedr installs on.

Does the recap spoil the book?

No. It only ever describes pages you have already read, never anything ahead of you.

Can I read the same book on another device?

Reedr keeps books on the device you added them to. Add the file on each device you read on; your place is kept separately on each.
