Reedr: FB2 Reader

Reedr is a free, independent reading app for FictionBook 2 files. It opens books you already have and that you are entitled to read. It supplies no books of its own and no catalogue, and it is your responsibility to hold the rights to whatever you add.

What the recap is, and is not

The chapter recap is generated by software, on your device, from the pages you have already read. It is an aid to memory and it can be incomplete, can flatten a detail, and can name a character in a way the book does not. Treat it as a reminder, not as the text. Where a recap and the book disagree, the book is right.

Recaps written by Apple’s on-device language model require a device with Apple Intelligence and iOS 26. On other devices Reedr assembles a recap from the book’s own sentences, which reads differently. Apple determines which devices support Apple Intelligence and when its model is ready to run.
