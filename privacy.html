Privacy Policy

Reedr: FB2 Reader

Reedr is a reader for FictionBook 2 (.fb2) books. You add books yourself, from Files, iCloud Drive, Mail, Safari or another app’s share sheet. When you add one, Reedr copies it into its own storage on this device so that the book stays available to you even if the original file moves. Books, covers, your place in each book and your typography choices are held on this device, in the app’s private container, and they are included in your device backup if you back the device up.

How recaps are made

Chapter recaps are produced on this device. On an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device language model reads the part of the chapter you have already passed and writes the recap; the model runs locally and the text of your book is not sent anywhere for it. On a device without Apple Intelligence, Reedr assembles the recap by selecting sentences from the book itself and by recognising the names in them with Apple’s on-device natural-language tools. Recaps are held in memory while you read and discarded when you close the book.

Network

Reedr works with no network connection. It has no account to create, no sign-in, and it contacts no server of the developer’s. The App Store handles the download and any review you choose to leave, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Control and deletion

Swipe a book away in the library to remove it, its cover and your place in it from this device. Deleting the app removes everything Reedr has stored. Files you added remain wherever you originally kept them, and are yours to delete separately.

Support and this site

Support is by email, and it carries only what you choose to write in it. Please describe a problem rather than attaching a book you do not hold the rights to share. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes visitor connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
